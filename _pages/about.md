I am a PhD student in the Department of Economics at Michigan State
University. My research is in microeconomic theory, with a focus on
information economics and on inequality and fairness.

## Research

My work primarily focuses on fairness and inequality, using tools from information economics
to better understand and address issues in these areas.
My current papers combine information economics with machine learning, with
applications to lending and intergenerational mobility.

## Teaching

I have taught Intermediate and Introductory Microeconomics as the
instructor of record at MSU, and I am a 2026-27 fellow at the Center for
Teaching and Learning Innovation. See my [CV]({{ base_path }}/cv/) for
the full list.
